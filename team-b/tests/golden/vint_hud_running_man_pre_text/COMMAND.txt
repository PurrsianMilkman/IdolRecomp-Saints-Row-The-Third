Why this document (enumerated and measured, not assumed): all 159 distinct .vint_doc files in
interface_startup.vpp_pc were walked with a throwaway probe; the HUD documents' bitmap images were matched
against the sheet sub-image registry and texture records. Almost every HUD image is a bitmap-SHEET
sub-image (mode 1, Sec12.1), not a standalone texture. Candidates:
  * hud.vint_doc (the main HUD): 134 bitmaps, but 7 mask elements (mask pass NOT modelled - its masked
    siblings would be drawn unmasked), 1 clip, 12 `document` sub-instances (not instanced), 6
    bitmap_circles, 5 image names in no sheet/texture, and in its as-loaded state full-screen backdrop/
    vignette layers cover most of the frame. Renders, but the frame is dominated by unmodelled behaviour -
    a poor regression target.
  * hud_running_man.vint_doc (CHOSEN): 297 records, 60 bitmaps, 28 groups, 2 text, NO mask, NO clip, NO
    sub-documents, NO unresolvable image (28 distinct images, all sheet sub-images: 27 on ui_bms_rm -
    page in interface.vpp_pc > ui_bms_rm.cvbm_pc, 1024x1024 - and ui_blank on ui_bms_00 - page in
    misc.vpp_pc > interface-backend.cpeg_pc), and it exercises rotation (11 rotated elements, e.g.
    fire_panda), scale, tint (exit_bg drawn with colour (150,47,103,255) from a tinted ui_blank), a
    visible=false cull (do_nothing_txt) and a text PLACEHOLDER (timer_txt). Every bitmap stores source_se
    explicitly AFTER image, and every drawn quad's corners equal the element's query-time screen_nw/
    screen_se box (unscaled_size-based) - an independent consistency check between Sec7 and Sec12.1 that
    the stdout records per element.

Why --tables patch_compressed.vpp_pc in front of misc_tables.vpp_pc: patch_compressed carries a 63-row
bitmap_sheets.xtbl but only 8 per-sheet <sheet>.xtbl files (a partial overlay); misc_tables has the 55-row
base set. The command layers them (first archive holding a FILE wins - a CHOSEN layering rule, the
engine's own archive precedence is not modelled), giving 63 sheets / 1,148 sub-images. The rendered frame
is identical with misc_tables alone (all of this document's images are base-set sub-images).

What is REAL vs CHOSEN vs stubbed in this frame (also printed in the command's own stdout banner):
  REAL      - every element, property, sheet rectangle and texel; the image-setter side effects; the cull,
              colour packing (x255 truncated, no clamp), composition, sibling order and quad geometry.
  CHOSEN    - document canvas 1280x720 (--doc-size; the source of document +0x5a0/+0x5a4 is not
              specced) with a 1280x720 target, so the screen fit is the identity; fit to height (Sec5.1
              HIGH); alpha blending for every render_mode (callback +0x18 semantics OPEN); linear/clamp
              sampler; no D3D9 half-pixel compensation; clear colour (40,40,48); sRGB colour decode OFF
              (Sec12.4 context byte +0x09 - HIGH per Sec11 item 9 that it stays 0, still CHOSEN) and sRGB
              texture decode OFF (UI sampler state untraced) - both toggleable (--srgb-colour/--srgb-texture
              on); custom_source_coords absent = clear (bitmap's constructor default not given; this
              document never stores it).
  STUBBED   - `text`: a magenta 24x12 outline at the composed origin is a PLACEHOLDER, NOT real text (the
              layout/glyph stream that would draw it is not specced). `group`: draws nothing - that IS
              the real behaviour (Sec12.3, a literal empty stub).
A future pass that settles any CHOSEN item above (canvas size, blend modes, sRGB, sampler, half-pixel) or
implements real text will legitimately change this baseline's pixels - an intended re-freeze, not a
regression.
