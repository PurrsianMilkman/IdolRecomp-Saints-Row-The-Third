## §2.1 `Binding_Set` row — per-set vs global array; set merge; `Common_Bindings`

- The four runtime arrays are `{data pointer, capacity, count}` triples: General `0x01412510/14/18`, On_Foot `0x014183A0/A4/A8`, Satellite `0x0141E230/34/38`, Driving `0x014240C0/C4/C8`; slot stride `0xFC0`.
